Manifesto

A New Standard of Feinkost

“A new standard of Feinkost has arrived in Berlin. Maison Asili masterfully bridges traditions, bringing the soul of rare African heritage botanicals into a refined Afro-Gourmet Fusion with exceptional flavours from around the world. Our artisanal, 100% natural cold-pressed specialty oils and premium spice blends are crafted without compromise. Designed for the passionate home chef and discerning soul food lover, they elevate everyday cuisine into an extraordinary culinary experience.”

Our Guiding Pillars

Rooted in Heritage. Crafted Without Compromise.

Every jar, tin, and bottle embodies our devotion to African agronomy, Berlin craftsmanship, and culinary integrity.

African Botanical Heritage

Direct partnerships with cooperative family growers across Kenya, Ethiopia, and Tanzania protect centuries of botanical intelligence and native biodiversity.

Artisanal Craftsmanship

Milled and bottled in small micro-batches in our Berlin Atelier. We refuse chemical stabilizers, fillers, or high-heat pressing to safeguard therapeutic volatiles.
