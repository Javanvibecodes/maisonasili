CHAPTER 01
2001 — 2006

From the Hearth of the Uckermark

In the quiet expanses of Brandenburg's Uckermark, long before Berlin became a worldwide gastronomical center, the journey began at a restored old farmhouse. A traditional clay oven bakery and oil mill stood side by side here, where baking and culinary oil extraction were practiced not as industry, but as elemental alchemy: raw fire, regional flour, pure spring water, and stone-cold pressing.

Every harvest cycle taught patience. Slow sourdough fermentations lasting up to 36 hours and small-batch seed pressing revealed how pure soil integrity directly translates into sensory depth. It was here that our exacting standards for raw materials were permanently codified.

CHAPTER 02
2006 — 2014

Into the Kinetic Heart of Kreuzberg

By the mid-2000s, the rural quietude met the creative velocity of Berlin-Kreuzberg. Establishing morning presence at weekly farmers’ markets and artisanal halls, our sourdough loaves and specialty oils quickly found an impassioned circle of connoisseurs, neighborhood elders, and innovative chefs.

Before dawn, loaves emerged from wood ovens, packed alongside bottles of freshly pressed walnut and pumpkinseed oils. Kreuzberg was unfiltered, multicultural, and fiercely authentic—a crucible that demanded undeniable quality and uncompromising craft.

CHAPTER 03
2012 — 2019

Recognized by Der Feinschmecker

3× FEINSCHMECKER AWARD

Selected among the premier culinary ateliers in the Federal Republic.

Critical national acclaim soon followed our quiet consistency. Germany’s leading gastronomy and epicurean journal, Der Feinschmecker, repeatedly highlighted our atelier for unmatched purity, artisan integrity, and reverence for traditional extraction techniques.

Berlin’s emerging wave of Michelin-starred dining rooms and private embassies turned to our pantry for table services, pairing our aromatic cold-pressed oils with contemporary culinary tasting menus.

Michelin Kitchens Permanent fixtures in Berlin’s tasting menus.
Diplomatic Menus Curated provisions for international delegations.
CHAPTER 04
2019 — 2022

Honoring the Legacy: From SoLuna Brot & Oel to Maison Asili

An evolutionary transformation rooted in profound continuity. The heritage forged over two decades under the name SoLuna Brot & Oel evolved into its natural, elevated expression as Maison Asili—expanding our culinary horizon from regional borders to the global African diaspora.

The Meaning of 'Asili'

In Swahili, Asili signifies origin, ancestry, essence, and nature. It embodies the truth that fine food is an ancestral gift that connects land, lineage, and the human table.

CHAPTER 05
2022 & BEYOND

A New Chapter, A Wider World: The Afro-Gourmet Dialogue

Maison Asili now bridges two vibrant culinary worlds: the precision and connoisseur culture of Berlin, and the unparalleled biodiversity of the African continent—from the volcanic soils of Mount Kenya to the aromatic spice archipelagos of Zanzibar.

We collaborate with multi-generational smallholder farming families, wild harvesters, and regenerative botanical cooperatives. Every provision in our archive is traceable, unblended, and hand-selected for extraordinary complexity.

DIRECT ETHICAL SOURCING

100% single-origin provenance, preserving fair value equity at origin while maintaining European certified feinkost standards.
